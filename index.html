Our Story

A Peaceful Retreat
in the Heart of Nature

Nestled in the lush, misty hills of Kalupahana, Little Heaven HomeStay & Villa is your serene getaway from the bustle of everyday life. Surrounded by verdant greenery and the gentle sounds of nature, our property offers a truly authentic Sri Lankan highland experience.

Whether you're a solo traveller seeking solitude, a couple looking for a romantic escape, or a family on a nature adventure — we promise warm hospitality, comfort, and memories that last a lifetime.
